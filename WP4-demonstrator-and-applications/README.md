# WP4 · Safety demonstrator and applications

Notes on the contents of this folder. The tasks and their estimated duration are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

To show the method where it has a direct use: the safety analysis of a real product. The lead demonstrator, the measurement chain of a battery management system, is taken through a mock safety assessment that compares the table-based FMEDA the industry uses today with the coverage computed by CERTA: coverage claimed, diagnostic hardware needed and evidence produced. Two secondary cases, with different kinds of risk, show that the method is domain-neutral.

| Case | Role | Kind of risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cell voltage and current measurement chain of a battery management system | Lead demonstrator, ideally co-designed with an industrial partner | Functional safety |
| ECG front end | Secondary case | Patient safety (IEC 60601-1, ISO 14971) |
| Bridge sensor conditioning (strain gauge or thermistor) in an unattended measurement node | Secondary case | Data integrity |

The specific circuits are still to be refined. It ends at milestone G4: in the lead demonstrator, the computed coverage meets the safety target with less diagnostic hardware than the table-based FMEDA.
