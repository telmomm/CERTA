Of the 2,632 records, 1,882 come from the database searches and 750 only from citation and author tracking.

The search retrieves 23 of the 24 works of the control set. The remaining one (Stenbakken and Souders, 1987) has no DOI and will enter through backward citation tracking, which is pending: 99 references cited by five or more non-excluded records are not yet in the corpus.

The corpus spans 1963 to 2026.

| Up to 1979 | 38 | 15 |
| 1980–1989 | 89 | 35 |
| 1990–1999 | 454 | 97 |
| 2000–2009 | 678 | 142 |
| 2010–2019 | 678 | 139 |
| 2020–2026 | 695 | 40 |

About 1,480 records are conference papers and 1,065 are journal articles; the rest are reviews, book chapters and magazine articles. The 2020–2026 period is inflated by the author searches, which bring in other research lines of the same groups.
