# WP1 · Benchmark and certification

Notes on the contents of this folder. The tasks and their estimated duration are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

To build the open benchmark on which every method is compared: benchmark circuits and fault universe in spicefault, enumeration of candidate tests, metrics with confidence intervals and adaptive Monte Carlo, and reimplemented baselines. It also defines the three application cases (circuit, specifications, measurement hardware and fault table with severities) that WP2 and WP4 use. It ends at milestone G1: the baselines reproduce the published figures, or the discrepancy is documented.

## Status

Not started. Requires milestone G0 (WP0).
