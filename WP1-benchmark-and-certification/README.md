# WP1 · Benchmark and certification

Notes on the contents of this folder. The tasks and their estimated duration are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

To build the open benchmark on which every method is compared: benchmark circuits and fault universe in spicefault, enumeration of candidate tests, metrics with confidence intervals and adaptive Monte Carlo, and reimplemented baselines. It ends at milestone G1: the baselines reproduce the published figures, or the discrepancy is documented.
