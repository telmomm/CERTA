# WP2 · Formulation and algorithms

Notes on the contents of this folder. The tasks and their estimated duration are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Objective

To formulate self-test design as a multi-objective selection problem under a hardware budget and to solve it: embedded cost model, fault priors and severities, greedy selection, exact integer linear programme and theoretical bound from the ambiguity groups. It ends at milestone G2: the greedy stays close to the optimum and risk weighting changes the selection.
